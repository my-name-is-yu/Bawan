## Redistribution and translation output

If you redistribute the model or a derived model (including bundling or mirroring
weights), provide a copy of the PLaMo agreement, require recipients to comply,
retain the required attribution, and follow Article 4. Bawan currently downloads
the model directly from PFN and does not redistribute weights.

Under Article 5, translation output normally needs no PLaMo attribution, but
publication of all or a substantial part of a book, paper or similar standalone
long-form work requires an acknowledgment of PLaMo's use. When providing or
publishing outputs, also disclose that models trained on those outputs are
subject to the PLaMo agreement. Rights in input text and third-party material
remain the user's responsibility.
